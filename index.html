About

Front Range Prototype Lab is a small, flexible machine shop focused on custom, one-off, and short-run projects. Whether you’re an engineer, inventor, small business, or hobbyist, we specialize in turning rough ideas into precise, reliable parts and assemblies.

Bring us a sketch on a napkin or a fully dimensioned drawing—we’ll meet you where you are. With decades of hands-on experience in machining, fabrication, and prototyping, we can help you refine your idea, troubleshoot technical challenges, and build something that actually works.

Our mission is simple: partner with you to transform your vision into a precise, real-world solution you can trust.

How It Works

1. Share Your Idea

Send us a sketch, drawing, or description of what you need. We’ll talk through your goals, constraints, and timelines.

2. Design & Plan

We help refine the concept, confirm dimensions and materials, and agree on an approach that fits your budget.

3. Build & Fabricate

We machine, weld, and assemble your parts to spec, keeping you in the loop as needed.

4. Test & Deliver

We test fit and function, make final adjustments, and hand off a ready-to-use prototype or finished product.

Services

Concept to Prototype

Have an idea but not sure how to build it? We can help with layout, dimensions, material choices, and practical design so it can be manufactured and tested.

Precision Machining

Custom one-off parts, replacement components, small runs, and special fixtures—machined to your drawings or created from a concept we develop together.

Welding & Fabrication

TIG/MIG welding and fabrication of brackets, frames, mounts, test stands, and more. We handle cutting, fitting, and final assembly.

Testing & Refinement

We don’t just hand you parts—we help you test fit and function and can make adjustments so your design is ready for the real world.
